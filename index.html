GATE Examination Instructions
Official TCS iON Candidate Guidelines
1. General Instructions
  • Total duration of examination is 180 minutes (3 hours).
  • The clock will be set at the server. A countdown timer in the top right corner displays remaining time.
  • When timer reaches zero, the examination ends automatically. You are not required to end or submit your exam manually unless finished early.

3. Marking Scheme & Negative Marks
  • MCQ (1 Mark): +1 for correct answer, -0.33 (⅓ mark) for incorrect answer.
  • MCQ (2 Marks): +2 for correct answer, -0.66 (⅔ mark) for incorrect answer.
  • MSQ (1 or 2 Marks): Full marks for all correct choices, ZERO negative marks. Partial credit is NOT given.
  • NAT (1 or 2 Marks): Numerical input. ZERO negative marks.
4. Virtual Calculator & NAT Rules
  • Physical scientific calculators are not allowed. Use the virtual calculator (C or topbar icon).
  • In strict mode, NAT responses must be entered via the on-screen numeric keypad using the mouse.
